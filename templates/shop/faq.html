Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products and services

🛒 Orders

How do I place an order?

Browse our products, select your desired items, add them to your cart, and proceed to checkout. Fill in your delivery details and choose your payment method.

Can I modify or cancel my order?

Yes, you can modify or cancel your order within 2 hours of placing it. Contact us immediately with your order ID.

How do I track my order?

Visit the Track Order page and enter your order ID to see your delivery status.

🚚 Delivery

How long does delivery take?

Orders are delivered within 2 business days. For Nairobi CBD, delivery is within 24 hours.

Do you deliver outside Nairobi?

Yes, we deliver to all parts of Kenya. Delivery to upcountry locations takes 2-3 business days.

Is delivery free?

Delivery is free for orders over KES 4,500. For orders below this amount, delivery fees vary by location.

💳 Payment

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept M-Pesa and Pay on Delivery. Online payments via Pesapal are also available.

Is my payment secure?

Yes, all payments are processed through secure gateways. We do not store your payment information.

📱 Products

Are your products genuine?

Yes, all our products are 100% genuine Oraimo products sourced from authorized distributors.

What is the warranty period?

All products come with a 12-month warranty against manufacturing defects.

Can I return a product?

Yes, you can return products within 14 days of delivery. See our Returns Policy for more details.
